# Mathematical resources

These are source pins, not adopted courses, verified learner access, or completed work. Official public course landing pages were inspected October 26, 2026. Local topic mappings below are planning judgments based on their public scope; precise reading/problem assignments require inspecting the relevant sections.

## Initial primary sources

- **Proof and discrete mathematics (1c/1d):** [MIT 6.042J, Mathematics for Computer Science, Spring 2015](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-042j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2015/). Its open textbook and public syllabus provide the first source for proof methods, discrete structures, counting, and discrete probability. Our direct-proofs lesson uses original examples and exercises.
- **Single-variable calculus (2a/2b):** [MIT 18.01SC, Fall 2010](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01sc-single-variable-calculus-fall-2010/). Pin this edition when selecting derivatives, applications, integration, and series material.
- **Multivariable/vector calculus (2c/2e):** [MIT 18.02SC, Fall 2010](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-02sc-multivariable-calculus-fall-2010/).
- **Linear algebra (2d):** [MIT 18.06SC, Fall 2011](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/).
- **ODEs and transforms (3a/3b):** [MIT 18.03SC, Fall 2011](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-03sc-differential-equations-fall-2011/). Use specific sections; this is not a complete source for all later PDE/control topics.
- **Real analysis (4a):** [MIT 18.100A, Fall 2020](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-100a-real-analysis-fall-2020/).
- **Probability and statistics (4c/4d):** [MIT 18.05, Spring 2022](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/). Distinguish course-specific R activities from local Python reference work.
- **Groups and algebra (5b, partial support for 5a):** [MIT 18.701, Algebra I, Fall 2010](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-701-algebra-i-fall-2010/). This pin does not establish coverage of the entire rings/fields/Galois path.
