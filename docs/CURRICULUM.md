# Mathematics curriculum

This is the topic blueprint. Stable IDs connect phase maps, the study plan, projects, and progress. It is a coverage framework, not a claim that complete courses or graduate lessons have been authored. Each broad topic needs smaller assessed modules before its full gate can be met.

Prerequisites describe conceptual dependencies for this plan, not university enrollment requirements. Topic-level prerequisites can be satisfied for the relevant subsection; record scope and gaps explicitly. See [resources](../RESOURCES.md) and [study order](../STUDY_PLAN.md).

## Phase 1: Foundations and mathematical reasoning

[Phase map](../notes/phase-1-foundations/README.md).

### 1a Arithmetic and algebra

**Prerequisites:** Entry diagnostic; no assumed mastery.

Manipulate fractions, powers, equations, inequalities, and units; justify restrictions on division and roots.

**Evidence target:** Explain the definitions and assumptions, solve or prove representative new problems, and complete an authored timed assessment plus learner reflection. Detailed lesson sets and rubrics remain to author.

### 1b Functions, trigonometry, and complex numbers

**Prerequisites:** 1a

Analyze domains, inverses, composition, graphs, exponential/logarithmic functions, trigonometry, and rectangular/polar complex arithmetic.

**Evidence target:** Explain the definitions and assumptions, solve or prove representative new problems, and complete an authored timed assessment plus learner reflection. Detailed lesson sets and rubrics remain to author.

### 1c Logic, sets, and proof

**Prerequisites:** 1a; function notation from 1b for later sections.

Use quantifiers, set operations, functions/relations, direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction, induction, and counterexamples.

**Evidence target:** Explain the definitions and assumptions, solve or prove representative new problems, and complete an authored timed assessment plus learner reflection. Detailed lesson sets and rubrics remain to author, except the introductory direct-proofs module below.

### 1d Discrete mathematics and combinatorics

**Prerequisites:** 1a, 1c

Count with bijections, combinations, inclusion-exclusion, recurrences, and elementary graph arguments.

**Evidence target:** Explain the definitions and assumptions, solve or prove representative new problems, and complete an authored timed assessment plus learner reflection. Detailed lesson sets and rubrics remain to author.

### 1e Mathematical computing

**Prerequisites:** 1a; relevant mathematics alongside each calculation.

Use Python, arrays, symbolic expressions, plotting, seeds, tolerances, and reproducible notebooks; distinguish exploration from proof.

**Evidence target:** Explain the definitions and assumptions, solve or prove representative new problems, and complete an authored timed assessment plus learner reflection. Detailed lesson sets and rubrics remain to author.
