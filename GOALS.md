# Mathematical goals

Established October 26, 2026 from the requested repository overhaul. This is a learning framework, with no claimed course completion or degree equivalence.

- **Engineering mathematics:** translate a physical or engineering question into equations, solve or approximate them, and check dimensions, assumptions, error, and stability.
- **Applied mathematics:** choose and analyze models, algorithms, inference methods, and optimization strategies; compare them against a baseline.
- **Theoretical mathematics:** derive results from precise definitions and hypotheses, understand convergence and structure, and explain where the result applies.
- **Abstract mathematics:** work with algebraic and topological structures, construct proofs and counterexamples, and recognize useful connections across subjects.
- **Mathematical communication and computation:** write reproducible derivations, proofs, notebooks, and project reports that distinguish exact claims from numerical evidence.

These areas overlap. Proof belongs in applied work, and examples/computation belong in abstract study. The [curriculum](docs/CURRICULUM.md) provides broad coverage; [the study plan](STUDY_PLAN.md) turns it into a manageable route.

## Relationship to other study

The `physics` repository supplies the model for organization and tutoring. It can supply application questions in mechanics, optics, materials, and quantum theory. This repository also supports mathematics independent of those applications. Do not inherit a physics-specific credential route, career goal, or weekly budget as a new math commitment.

Use existing work across repositories as evidence only after inspecting its actual scope and assistance. Link it instead of claiming that a matching course title establishes mastery. The current starting level, weekly allocation, preferred route, and adopted textbook editions remain unrecorded.
